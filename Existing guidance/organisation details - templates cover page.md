# Extended producer responsibility for packaging: templates for organisation details 

If you need to submit your organisation details under extended producer responsibility for packaging these CSV templates will help you to structure your data correctly.

If you’re an organisation that supplies packaging to consumers and businesses in the UK, you may have to take action to comply with extended producer responsibility (EPR) for packaging.

[Find out if you’re affected by EPR for packaging](/government/admin/detailed-guides/1469917)

You may need to submit information about your organisation, including things like:

* address
* turnover
* packaging activities
* contact details

You may also need to submit brand and partner details, depending on the structure of your organisation.

If you do have to report this data you can use these CSV spreadsheets as templates. 

There is guidance that explains [how to create your organisation data file for extended producer responsibility](/government/admin/publications/1464715). This covers:

* what the column headers mean
* which columns you'll have to complete
* the codes you'll need to enter 

^ Do not remove any columns from the CSV files, even if you do not have to fill them in.

You can [give feedback about all the EPR guidance](https://defragroup.eu.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_9LUU4aCQonmYe1M). Let us know if anything is unclear or incorrect.
