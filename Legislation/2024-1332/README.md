## Quick Reference

### Producer Classes (Part 2)
- **Brand owner** (reg 16): Person whose brand appears on packaging
- **Packer/filler** (reg 17): Person who puts goods into packaging
- **Importer** (reg 18): Person responsible for importing packaging
- **Distributor** (reg 19): Manufactures/imports unfilled packaging
- **Online marketplace operator** (reg 20): Controls online marketplace
- **Service provider** (reg 21): Supplies reusable packaging by hire/loan
- **Seller** (reg 22): Supplies filled packaging to final user

### Threshold Criteria (reg 24)
- **Large producer**: Turnover >£2m AND handles >50 tonnes packaging/year
- **Small producer**: Turnover >£2m OR handles >50 tonnes (but not both)

### Key Definitions (reg 2)
- **Household packaging**: Primary/shipment packaging likely disposed in household bins
- **Packaging waste**: Packaging that has become waste
- **PRN/PERN**: Packaging waste recovery/export recovery note
