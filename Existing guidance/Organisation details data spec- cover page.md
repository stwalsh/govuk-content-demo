# Organisation details: how to create your file for extended producer responsibility (EPR) for packaging

This guidance explains how to report organisation details in order to register for extended producer responsibility (EPR) for packaging. This is a legal obligation for organisations that are affected by EPR for packaging. 

If you’re an organisation that supplies packaging to consumers and businesses in the UK, you may have to take action to comply with extended producer responsibility (EPR) for packaging.

[Find out if you’re affected by EPR for packaging](/government/admin/detailed-guides/1469917)

As part of [registration for EPR for packaging](/government/admin/detailed-guides/1634475), you need to submit information about your organisation, including things like:

* address
* turnover
* packaging activities
* contact details
* organisation size

This document explains what information you need to supply and how to structure it. 


There are [templates to help you structure your data](/government/admin/publications/1464798)

You may also need to submit data about your packaging. There's separate guidance explaining [how to create your packaging data file](/government/admin/publications/1463057).

Submit your data using the ['report packaging data' service](/government/admin/detailed-guides/1472620).
