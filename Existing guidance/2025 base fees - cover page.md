Extended Producer Responsibility for Packaging: 2025 base fees

Base fees for year 1 (2025 to 2026) of Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) for packaging.

New regulations apply to all UK organisations that import or supply packaging. The regulations mean that these organisations may be responsible for the costs of dealing with packaging waste. This is called extended producer responsibility (EPR) for packaging.

This document provides the base fees for year 1 of EPR for packaging, in advance of invoices sent to producers in October 2025.

Illustrative base fees were published in [August](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/extended-producer-responsibility-for-packaging-illustrative-base-fees-august-2024),
[September](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/extended-producer-responsibility-for-packaging-illustrative-base-fees) and [December 2024](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/epr-for-packaging-updated-illustrative-base-fees-december-2024).
